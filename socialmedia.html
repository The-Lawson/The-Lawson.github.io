Social Media Posts

In high school, I was elected "Social Media Person" for Yearbook Club and Improv Club. That is when my enjoyment of social media management began. Over the years, I've learned how to take and edit photos, create posts, and write engaging captions for multiple accounts. Because I have managed so many different accounts, I have learned how to adjust my strategies for varius audiences.
